Reflection 

How do I approach designing software?
I approach designing software by first planning out the project in great detail and understanding what the project requires of me. I clearly define the problem I am trying to solve and identify the key components necessary to build the solution. In this project, I envisioned the final 3D scene and then decomposed it into smaller, more manageable pieces, such as the tree, ground plane, and lighting. I paid attention to creating a design that was functional but also aesthetically pleasing. I also applied principles like modularity so that each part of the scene could be developed and tested in isolation but fit seamlessly into the larger design.

What new design skills has your work on the project helped you to craft?
The project introduced me to a number of new design skills, which I did not know existed, especially in computational graphics. I learned how to apply materials, textures, and lighting strategically to create depth and realism in a 3D environment. Balancing aesthetic considerations with technical limitations was a valuable experience, such as rendering performance. I also developed my skill for the visualization of abstract conceptions into code and graphical visualizations. Another important skill I developed pertains to how geometric transformations (like scaling, rotation, and translation) interact with the virtual environment.

Which design process did you follow on your project work?
I followed the iterative design process in constructing my 3D scene. This process started off with conceptualization, where I sketched the components of the scene and decided on their arrangement. Then, I moved to implementation, starting with the most basic shapes-for example, cylinders and cones for the tree. From that point forward, I began to layer on details, such as textures and colors, testing frequently to make sure each addition enhanced the overall composition. I also spent some time experimenting with different lighting positions and intensities, as this was one of the keys to achieving the desired reflections and shadows. Testing and getting feedback at every stage has allowed me to incrementally refine this scene.

How could tactics from your design approach be applied in future work?
The tactics I used in this project, such as breaking down the design into smaller tasks and iterating on each component, are applicable to almost any software development project. Whether designing user interfaces, creating simulations, or developing backend systems, modularity, iterative refinement, and testing can help produce efficient and effective designs. Moreover, the experience of working with visual aesthetics and balancing functionality against creativity will help in creating engaging and user-friendly software in the future.

How do I approach program development?
My approach to the development of programs involves starting with a solid foundation and building incrementally. I write small, testable pieces of code and then integrate them to form the complete program. First, I did the basic elements of the scene, such as a tree and ground plane, making sure they rendered properly before adding textures and lighting. In this way, I can handle the complexity of the process and make sure that I will be able to find out where the problem is if something goes wrong.

What new development strategies did you use while working on your 3D scene?
The other strategies that I integrated while doing this project are playing with the light and the properties of materials for advanced visual enhancements, an iterative approach of developing by testing little modifications every now and then to observe what is working right with that modification, such as object size, direction of light, among other attributes. Another approach I did was leveraging the documentation or other resources to make out how a particular Graphics API does work and how one could employ them in practice.

How does iteration factor into your development?
Iteration played a key role in the development of my work. Any given component of this scene underwent a multitude of rounds of refinement. For example, several reiterations in the tree's proportion achieved its realistic appearance as well as complemented other elements within this scene. Similarly, many positions and intensities were tried before finding a spot with just the right mix between light, dark, shadows, and reflection. With every iteration, it added more and more value to the whole scene by informing me on what still needs to be focused on.

How has your approach to developing code evolved throughout the milestones, which led you to the project's completion? My approach to coding has become more systematic and organized. During the earlier milestones, I focused on just getting basic functionality working but, as the project progressed, I gave greater importance to writing modular and reusable code. I also started to develop a habit of testing frequently and documenting my progress, making debugging and refining the code way easier. At the end of the project, I felt much more confident in my ability to attack complex problems in a methodical and efficient way.

How can computer science help me in reaching my goals?
Computer science provides the foundational knowledge and tools I need to achieve my goals in software development and problem-solving. This project reinforced how powerful computational graphics can be in creating engaging visualizations, which is a skill I aim to leverage in my future career. Whether I'm designing interactive applications or visualizing data, the principles and techniques I've learned will be invaluable.

How do computational graphics and visualizations give you new knowledge and skills that can be applied in your future educational pathway?
This project introduced me to some important concepts in computational graphics, such as 3D modeling, lighting, and texture mapping, which can also be applied to virtual reality, the creation of games, and even data visualization. These skills are particularly relevant for courses involving simulations, animations, or user interface design. It will also enhance my ability to construct educational tools and presentations, making difficult concepts easier to understand by simplifying them visually.

How do computational graphics and visualizations provide new knowledge and skills that you could utilize in your future professional path?
In a professional context, computational graphics are widely used in industries ranging from entertainment to scientific research. The skills I’ve gained, such as creating 3D environments and optimizing visual elements, will be beneficial for roles in game development, virtual reality, or software engineering. Additionally, understanding how to manipulate and render 3D objects can help in designing user-friendly interfaces or creating compelling marketing materials.

Overall, this project was both enjoyable and challenging. It allowed me to apply a wide range of skills while learning new techniques in computational graphics. Despite some hurdles, such as fine-tuning the lighting and reflections, I found the process incredibly rewarding. I look forward to expanding on these skills in future projects!
